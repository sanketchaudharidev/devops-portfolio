## ⚙️ `lets-scroll` Generation Rules for Google Veo:
1. **Always use Image-to-Video (I2V):** Feed the Scene `.jpg` as the starting frame.
2. **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (Landscape)
3. **Motion Speed:** Set motion/camera speed to **Slow / Smooth** (fast moves cause motion blur during scrubbing).
4. **Resolution:** 1080p or 4K.
5. **Saved Destination:** Drop the resulting `.mp4` clips into `public/scenes/`.
