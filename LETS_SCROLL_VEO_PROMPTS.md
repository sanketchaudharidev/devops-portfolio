## 🛠️ Instructions for Generating in Google Veo:
1. Open **Google Veo** (or Runway Gen-3 / Kling).
2. Choose **Image-to-Video (I2V)**.
3. Upload the corresponding scene `.jpg` from `public/scenes/`.
4. Paste the **Veo Video Motion Prompt**.
5. Set aspect ratio to **16:9 (Landscape)** and motion to **Slow/Smooth**.
6. Save the downloaded `.mp4` into `public/scenes/` using the specified filename.
